Nine ways Oklahoma could look on a phone

Each direction is a complete, operable design system built on the real universe — 503 S&P 500 names, real closes, and the same metrics the production page computes. The first eight each commit to one thesis; the ninth is the synthesis of four of them. They are throwaway design instruments, not implementation candidates: nothing in web/, oklahoma/ or data/ has been touched.
